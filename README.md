# Yumlytics

Yumlytics is an application that leverages artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze YouTube restaurant review videos. With our app, you can explore a curated list of restaurants along with summarized opinions from popular content creators, making it easier to find the perfect spot for your next meal.

## Features
- **YouTube Video Analysis**: Automatically processes videos and extracts reviews about restaurants.
- **Restaurant Directory**: Browse restaurants with summarized ratings and opinions from reviewers.
- **Search and Filters**: Easily find restaurants by name, location, or category.
- **Review Comparisons**: See what different YouTubers think about the same restaurant.

## How It Works
1. The app fetches links to YouTube restaurant reviews.
2. Videos are analyzed using AI to extract key insights (e.g., opinions on food, atmosphere, or pricing).
3. Users can browse the results in a clean and organized list of restaurants.
